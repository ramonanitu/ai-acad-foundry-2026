Libra Bank's complaints process (see
[complaints-intake-procedure.md](complaints-intake-procedure.md)) exists to resolve
issues with products and services Libra Bank actually provides. It cannot
investigate or compensate a complaint about something outside that scope. Being
clear about scope matters as much as being clear about process: telling a customer
"we will look into it" for something the bank does not offer only delays an honest
answer.

**Libra Bank does not offer, and therefore cannot receive a product complaint
about:**

- **Student loans.** Libra Bank has no student loan product, subsidized or
  otherwise. A complaint referencing a Libra Bank "student loan" is almost always a
  mix-up with another lender or a government program, and staff should say so
  directly rather than opening a case.
- **Cryptocurrency trading or custody.** Libra Bank does not buy, sell, hold, or
  facilitate trading of cryptocurrency or other digital assets for retail
  customers.
- **Foreign-currency retail mortgages.** Libra Bank issues retail mortgages in RON
  only (see the mortgage product documentation); it does not originate or service
  mortgages denominated in EUR, USD, or CHF for retail customers.
- **Investment or brokerage advice.** Libra Bank does not offer a retail brokerage
  or investment advisory product; complaints about stock trading losses are out of
  scope.

**Handled by a partner, not directly by Libra Bank:**

- **Bancassurance products** (home, travel, or life insurance sold at a Libra Bank
  branch) are underwritten by a separate partner insurer. Libra Bank can log and
  forward a complaint about the sale itself (was it explained properly, was the
  right product recommended), which falls under "mis-selling / advice" in
  [complaints-taxonomy.md](complaints-taxonomy.md). But a complaint about a claim
  decision, a premium calculation, or policy wording belongs to the insurer's own
  complaints process, and Libra Bank will provide the insurer's contact details
  rather than investigate the claim itself.

When a complaint falls outside scope, staff still log it (see
[complaints-intake-procedure.md](complaints-intake-procedure.md), step 2) so the
volume is tracked, then close it with the reason "out of scope" and, where
possible, point the customer to the right organization.
