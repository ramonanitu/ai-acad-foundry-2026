The table below lists the internal service-level targets per complaint category,
under the current (2026) policy — see [complaints-policy-2026.md](complaints-policy-2026.md)
for how these targets changed from the 2025 policy. "Acknowledgment" is the deadline
to confirm receipt to the customer; "Resolution target" is the deadline for a final
decision, both measured in business days from the day the complaint is logged.

| Category | Acknowledgment | Resolution target | Owner team | Can extend? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fraud | Same business day | 5 business days | Fraud & Security | No |
| Card disputes | 2 business days | 10 business days | Cards Operations | Yes, +10 days if issuer network involved |
| Fees and charges | 2 business days | 15 business days | Retail Operations | No |
| Mis-selling / advice | 2 business days | 15 business days | Compliance | Yes, +10 days, must notify customer in writing |
| Service quality | 2 business days | 10 business days | Branch / channel manager | No |
| Mortgage and lending | 2 business days | 15 business days | Lending Operations | Yes, +10 days for cases needing external valuation |
| Data privacy | 2 business days | 15 business days | Data Protection Officer | No — statutory limit |
| Accessibility | 2 business days | 10 business days | Accessibility & Inclusion | No |
| Account closure | 2 business days | 15 business days | Compliance | Yes, +10 days |

If a resolution target is extended, Libra Bank must notify the customer in writing
before the original deadline, stating the new deadline and the reason. A complaint
that receives no substantive response within the resolution target (extended or not)
gives the customer the right to escalate externally — see
[complaints-escalation-procedure.md](complaints-escalation-procedure.md) and the
waiting-period rules in [complaints-ombudsman-2026.md](complaints-ombudsman-2026.md).

Fraud has no extension path by design: Libra Bank's Fraud & Security team must reach
a provisional decision (block, reimburse, or refer to criminal investigation) within
5 business days even if the underlying police report is still pending.
