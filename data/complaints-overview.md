title: What counts as a complaint at Libra Bank

A **complaint** at Libra Bank is any expression of dissatisfaction, written or oral,
about a product, a service, an employee, or a process, where the customer expects a
response or a remedy. This is broader than a simple question or a service request:
asking "why was I charged this fee?" is a query; saying "I was charged this fee and
I believe it is wrong, please refund it" is a complaint and must be logged as one.

Libra Bank distinguishes three related but different things, because they follow
different internal procedures:

- A **complaint** — dissatisfaction requiring investigation and a formal response.
  See [complaints-intake-procedure.md](complaints-intake-procedure.md).
- A **dispute** — a customer contests a specific transaction as incorrect or
  unauthorized (most commonly a card transaction). See
  [complaints-card-disputes-procedure.md](complaints-card-disputes-procedure.md).
- **Fraud** — the customer or the bank suspects a criminal act (stolen card,
  phishing, account takeover). See [complaints-fraud-vs-dispute.md](complaints-fraud-vs-dispute.md),
  which explains why fraud is routed differently and faster than an ordinary dispute.

Any customer-facing employee who receives a complaint, in any channel, is required
to log it in the complaints register the same business day — complaints are never
handled informally "off the books," even when the employee can resolve the issue on
the spot (for example, waiving a single fee). A same-day resolution still gets a
complaint record, closed as "resolved at first contact," because Libra Bank reports
aggregate complaint volumes to the National Bank of Romania (BNR) quarterly and an
unlogged complaint distorts that reporting.

This corpus covers who can complain, how, about what, within what timeframes, and
what happens if the customer is still unhappy after Libra Bank's internal process is
exhausted. It does not cover general customer service scripts or marketing
communications, which live in a separate internal knowledge base.
