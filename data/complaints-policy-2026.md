This is the current Libra Bank complaints handling policy, effective 1 January 2026.
It **supersedes the 2025 edition** ([complaints-policy-2025.md](complaints-policy-2025.md))
in full; where the two documents differ, this one governs for any complaint logged
on or after 1 January 2026.

What changed from 2025:

- Acknowledgment is now due within **2 business days** of receipt (was 5 business
  days), except fraud, which is acknowledged the same business day.
- Resolution targets are now **category-specific**, ranging from 5 business days
  (fraud) to 15 business days (fees, mis-selling, mortgage/lending, data privacy,
  account closure) — see the full breakdown in
  [complaints-sla-table.md](complaints-sla-table.md). This replaces the flat 30
  calendar day target used in 2025.
- **Email** (reclamatii@librabank.example.ro) is now a formal complaint channel,
  alongside app, call center, branch, and post — see
  [complaints-channels.md](complaints-channels.md).
- The waiting period before a customer can escalate externally to CSALB without a
  final decision was shortened; see
  [complaints-ombudsman-2026.md](complaints-ombudsman-2026.md) for the current
  figure, which is materially different from the 2025 figure and should not be
  confused with it.
- Extensions must now be flagged internally by the halfway point of the resolution
  window, not just before the deadline, so customers are told earlier if a case
  will run long.

This edition applies to every complaint logged from 1 January 2026 onward. A
complaint logged in December 2025 but still open on 1 January 2026 continues under
the 2025 targets it started with — policy changes are not applied retroactively to
open cases, only to newly logged ones.
