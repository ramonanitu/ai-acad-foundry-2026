This is the 2025 edition of Libra Bank's complaints handling policy, effective
1 January 2025 through 31 December 2025. It has been **superseded by the 2026
edition** (see [complaints-policy-2026.md](complaints-policy-2026.md)) and is kept
here for reference only — do not quote these figures for a complaint filed in 2026
or later.

Under the 2025 policy:

- Acknowledgment of a new complaint was due within **5 business days** of receipt.
- The general resolution target was **30 calendar days** from the day the complaint
  was logged, regardless of category — the 2025 policy did not yet differentiate
  targets by complaint category.
- Extensions were permitted up to an additional 15 calendar days, with written
  notice to the customer, for any category.
- Complaints could be filed through four channels: mobile app, call center, branch,
  and written post. Email as a formal channel was introduced only in the 2026
  edition.
- The right to escalate externally to CSALB arose once **45 calendar days** had
  passed from the complaint being logged without a final decision (see the 2025
  figure preserved in
  [complaints-ombudsman-2025.md](complaints-ombudsman-2025.md)).

This edition was retired to bring Libra Bank in line with a 2025 update to the
National Bank of Romania's consumer-complaints handling regulation, which required
faster acknowledgment, category-specific resolution targets, and a shorter path to
external escalation. The 2026 edition implements those changes.
