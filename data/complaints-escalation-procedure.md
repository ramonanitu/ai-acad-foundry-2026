If you are not satisfied with how Libra Bank has handled your complaint — or if the
resolution target passed with no substantive response — you can escalate in stages.
Follow these in order; skipping straight to the regulator without giving Libra Bank
the chance to respond internally can slow your case down, since the regulator will
usually send it back to us first.

1. **Ask for a supervisor review.** Reply to the decision letter (or call 0800 123
   400) and ask that a team lead review the original decision. This is free, and
   Libra Bank aims to respond to a supervisor review within 5 business days.

2. **Escalate to the Head of Complaints.** If the supervisor review does not change
   the outcome, write to complaints.escalation@librabank.example.ro with your
   tracking number and "Level 2 escalation" in the subject. The Head of Complaints
   personally reviews the file and responds within 10 business days.

3. **External referral — CSALB (Financial Consumer Ombudsman).** Once Libra Bank's
   internal process is exhausted, or the waiting period described in
   [complaints-ombudsman-2026.md](complaints-ombudsman-2026.md) has passed without
   resolution, you may refer the case to CSALB (Centrul de Soluționare Alternativă a
   Litigiilor în Domeniul Bancar), a free alternative dispute resolution body.
   CSALB requires your tracking number and Libra Bank's final decision letter (or
   proof that the resolution target passed with no response).

4. **External referral — ANPC.** For consumer-protection matters not specific to
   financial disputes (e.g., misleading advertising), you may also contact ANPC
   (Autoritatea Națională pentru Protecția Consumatorilor) in parallel with CSALB.

5. **Courts.** Referral to CSALB or ANPC does not remove your right to go to court
   at any point; it simply offers a faster, free alternative most customers try
   first.

Libra Bank does not charge for any step of this escalation process, and raising a
complaint or escalating it never affects your credit score or your existing
products.
