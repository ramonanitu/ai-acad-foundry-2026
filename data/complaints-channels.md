Libra Bank accepts complaints through five channels. All five carry equal weight —
using the mobile app does not get you a faster answer than a written letter, though
some channels are more convenient for attaching evidence.

1. **Mobile app** — Menu → Support → "File a complaint." Lets you attach documents
   (screenshots, receipts) directly and gives you a tracking number immediately.
2. **Call center** — 0800 123 400, free from Romanian numbers, available Mon–Sat
   08:00–20:00. The agent logs the complaint while you are on the line and reads
   your tracking number back to you before hanging up.
3. **Branch, in person** — any Libra Bank branch. Ask for a "complaint form"
   (formular de reclamație); you leave with a stamped copy and a tracking number.
4. **Email** — reclamatii@librabank.example.ro. Treated as received on the calendar
   day it arrives, even outside business hours; the acknowledgment clock (see
   [complaints-policy-2026.md](complaints-policy-2026.md)) starts the next business day.
5. **Written post** — Libra Bank, Complaints Department, Str. Exemplu nr. 1,
   București. Slower to log (postal delay), so the app or email is recommended
   whenever documents need to be attached.

**Not a valid channel:** social media comments or direct messages (Facebook,
Instagram, X). Libra Bank's social media team may reply publicly for goodwill, but
a message there is not logged as a formal complaint and does not start any SLA
clock. If you raise an issue on social media, the team will ask you to also file
through one of the five channels above.

Every complaint, regardless of channel, receives a **tracking number** in the format
`LB-CMP-YYYY-NNNNNN`. Keep this number: it is required to check status by phone or
app, and it is the reference the Financial Consumer Ombudsman (CSALB) or ANPC will
ask for if you escalate later (see [complaints-escalation-procedure.md](complaints-escalation-procedure.md)).
