Every logged complaint is assigned exactly one primary category from the list
below. The category determines which team investigates and which service-level
target applies (see the table in [complaints-sla-table.md](complaints-sla-table.md)).

- **Card disputes** — a specific card transaction is contested as wrong, duplicated,
  or unauthorized. Routed to the Cards Operations team.
- **Fraud** — suspected criminal activity: stolen card, phishing, account takeover,
  social engineering. Routed to the Fraud & Security team, always fast-tracked.
- **Fees and charges** — disagreement about a fee applied to an account, card, or
  loan (e.g., "I was charged an annual fee I didn't expect"). Routed to Retail
  Operations.
- **Mis-selling / advice** — the customer believes a product was recommended or
  sold in a misleading way, or that terms were not properly explained at sale.
  Routed to the Compliance team; these are never resolved by front-line staff alone.
- **Service quality** — slow service, unhelpful staff, long queues, unclear
  communication. Routed to the branch or channel manager involved.
- **Mortgage and lending** — issues specific to a mortgage or personal loan
  (disbursement delays, incorrect interest applied, early repayment disputes).
  Routed to the Lending Operations team.
- **Data privacy** — a request or complaint about how personal data was collected,
  used, or shared. Routed to the Data Protection Officer, who has a separate,
  regulator-mandated response clock that runs alongside the standard one.
- **Accessibility** — a customer with a disability could not access a service or
  channel as required. Routed to the Accessibility & Inclusion team.
- **Account closure / relationship exit** — disputes arising when Libra Bank closes
  or restricts an account. Routed to Compliance, given the regulatory sensitivity.

A complaint can touch more than one category in practice (a mis-sold card with a
disputed annual fee, for example). In that case staff pick the category that
represents the customer's primary grievance and note the secondary category in
free text — the SLA of the *primary* category governs the response deadline.
