---
title: Waiting period before CSALB escalation — 2025 rule
product: complaints
audience: retail
effective: 2025-01-01
version: 1
status: superseded
---

Under the rule in force during 2025, a customer could refer an unresolved
complaint to CSALB (the Financial Consumer Ombudsman) once **45 calendar days**
had passed from the date the complaint was logged with Libra Bank, without a final
written decision having been issued.

This 45-day figure was tied to the 2025 general resolution target of 30 calendar
days plus a standard 15-day extension allowance (see
[complaints-policy-2025.md](complaints-policy-2025.md)): the waiting period assumed
the bank might legitimately use the full extension before a decision was reached.

**This rule no longer applies.** It was replaced effective 1 January 2026 by a
shorter waiting period — see
[complaints-ombudsman-2026.md](complaints-ombudsman-2026.md) for the current figure.
This document is retained only so that complaints logged in 2025, which continue
under the policy they started with, can be assessed correctly. Do not use 45 days
as the waiting period for any complaint logged in 2026 or later; that would tell a
customer they have to wait roughly three times longer than the current rule
actually requires.
