Use this procedure when you recognize the merchant but believe a specific card
transaction is wrong — duplicated, wrong amount, goods never received, or a
subscription you already cancelled. If instead you do not recognize the transaction
at all or suspect your card details were stolen, use
[complaints-fraud-vs-dispute.md](complaints-fraud-vs-dispute.md) instead, which is
faster and follows a different path.

1. **Check the transaction first.** Open the app and confirm the merchant name,
   date, and amount. Pending transactions can take up to 5 business days to settle
   and sometimes resolve themselves (e.g., a duplicate authorization hold that
   drops off).

2. **Contact the merchant.** For non-delivery, wrong amount, or subscription
   issues, Libra Bank asks that you first try to resolve it directly with the
   merchant — most disputes are solved faster this way, and card networks require
   evidence that you tried before a chargeback is filed.

3. **File the dispute.** If the merchant does not resolve it within 7 calendar
   days, or refuses, file the dispute via the app (Transaction → "Dispute this
   transaction") or a channel from
   [complaints-channels.md](complaints-channels.md). Provide: the transaction
   reference, what you expected to happen, and any evidence (order confirmation,
   cancellation email, correspondence with the merchant).

4. **Provisional credit.** For disputes over 50 lei, Libra Bank applies a
   provisional credit to your account within 5 business days while the
   investigation continues, so you are not out of pocket during the process.

5. **Chargeback filed with the card network.** Cards Operations files a formal
   chargeback with Visa or Mastercard, who give the merchant's bank a chance to
   respond with counter-evidence.

6. **Outcome.** If the chargeback succeeds, the provisional credit becomes
   permanent. If it fails (the merchant provides valid proof, for example a
   signed delivery confirmation), the provisional credit is reversed and you are
   notified in writing with the merchant's evidence summarized.

7. **Still disagree?** Use [complaints-escalation-procedure.md](complaints-escalation-procedure.md).

The resolution target for card disputes is 10 business days from the day the
dispute is logged, extendable to 20 if the card network investigation is still
pending — see [complaints-sla-table.md](complaints-sla-table.md).
