---
title: How long complaint records are kept
product: complaints
audience: staff
effective: 2026-01-01
version: 2
---

Every complaint logged under
[complaints-intake-procedure.md](complaints-intake-procedure.md) is retained in the
complaints register for a minimum of **5 years** from the closing date, in line
with National Bank of Romania (BNR) record-keeping requirements for consumer
complaints. Fraud-related complaints, and any complaint that resulted in a goodwill
payment or a legal referral, are retained for **7 years** instead.

The record kept includes: the original complaint text, the tracking number, the
category assigned, all correspondence with the customer, the internal
investigation notes, the decision letter, and (where applicable) the goodwill
amount paid. This is retained regardless of the outcome — a rejected complaint is
kept exactly as long as an upheld one.

Closing a complaint (step 8 of the intake procedure) does not delete or archive it
out of reach: a closed complaint remains searchable by tracking number for the full
retention period, and can be reopened per
[complaints-satisfaction-survey.md](complaints-satisfaction-survey.md) without
losing the original record.

**Regulatory reporting.** Libra Bank submits aggregate complaint statistics to the
BNR quarterly: volumes by category, average resolution time against the targets in
[complaints-sla-table.md](complaints-sla-table.md), and the proportion escalated to
CSALB. This is aggregate and anonymized — no individual customer's complaint
details are shared with the regulator as part of routine quarterly reporting; only
a CSALB or court referral shares an individual case file, and only with the body
handling that specific case.

Customers may request a copy of their own complaint record at any time under data
protection rules; this request itself does not count as a new complaint and follows
the standard data-subject-access process, not the complaints process.
