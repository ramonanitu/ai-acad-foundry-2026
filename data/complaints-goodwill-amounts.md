This document sets the amounts Libra Bank pays once a complaint is confirmed
eligible for goodwill under
[complaints-eligibility-goodwill.md](complaints-eligibility-goodwill.md). Eligibility
and amount are decided separately: being eligible does not by itself tell you how
much you would receive, and these figures do not apply to anyone who does not meet
the eligibility conditions in the linked document.

**Administrative inconvenience** (no measurable financial loss):
- Minor (single missed communication, small delay under 5 business days): 100 lei.
- Moderate (repeated failures, delay of 5–20 business days, customer had to contact
  the bank more than twice about the same issue): 250 lei.
- Significant (delay over 20 business days, or the error required the customer to
  take time off work or travel to a branch unnecessarily): up to 500 lei, at the
  case owner's discretion.

**Proven financial loss:**
- Libra Bank reimburses the documented loss in full, plus a goodwill uplift of
  **3% of the documented financial loss, capped at 5,000 lei** per complaint.
- Example: a customer documents 800 lei in avoidable overdraft interest caused by a
  Libra Bank processing error. The 800 lei is refunded in full, and a further
  3% × 800 = 24 lei is added as goodwill, for a total credit of 824 lei.
- For losses above roughly 167,000 lei, the 3% uplift would exceed the 5,000 lei
  cap, so the cap applies instead of the percentage.

Goodwill payments are credited directly to the customer's Libra Bank current
account within 5 business days of the decision letter, and are not subject to any
account fee. They are not reported as taxable income by Libra Bank, though
customers with unusual tax situations should confirm independently.

Goodwill amounts are reviewed annually; this schedule is the 2026 version and
supersedes any figures quoted before 1 January 2026.
