title: Complaint intake procedure, step by step

This is the mandatory sequence every logged complaint follows, from the moment it
arrives to the moment it is closed. Skipping a step, or doing them out of order, is
a process breach even if the customer ends up satisfied.

1. **Receive.** The complaint arrives through one of the five channels in
   [complaints-channels.md](complaints-channels.md). The receiving employee captures
   the customer's account or card reference, a description in the customer's own
   words, and any attached evidence.

2. **Log.** The complaint is entered into the complaints register the same business
   day, generating a tracking number `LB-CMP-YYYY-NNNNNN`. This step is never
   skipped, including for issues resolved instantly (see
   [complaints-overview.md](complaints-overview.md) on "resolved at first contact").

3. **Categorize.** Assign the primary category from
   [complaints-taxonomy.md](complaints-taxonomy.md). The category fixes which team
   owns the case and which resolution target applies.

4. **Acknowledge.** Send the customer a written acknowledgment (app notification,
   email, or letter) within the deadline set in
   [complaints-sla-table.md](complaints-sla-table.md), confirming the tracking
   number and the expected resolution date.

5. **Investigate.** The owning team gathers evidence: transaction logs, call
   recordings, contracts, staff statements. For categories with an extension option,
   the team must flag by the halfway point of the target window if it looks like
   the deadline will be missed.

6. **Decide.** The team reaches one of three outcomes: uphold (refund, correct,
   apologize as appropriate), partially uphold, or reject with a documented reason.

7. **Communicate the decision.** The customer receives a written decision letter
   explaining the reasoning in plain language, referencing any goodwill payment
   (see [complaints-eligibility-goodwill.md](complaints-eligibility-goodwill.md) and
   [complaints-goodwill-amounts.md](complaints-goodwill-amounts.md)), and stating
   the customer's right to escalate if unsatisfied.

8. **Close.** The case is marked closed in the register with the outcome code.
   Closing does not delete the record — see
   [complaints-record-keeping.md](complaints-record-keeping.md).

9. **Survey.** The customer receives a short satisfaction survey; see
   [complaints-satisfaction-survey.md](complaints-satisfaction-survey.md) for how a
   dissatisfied customer can reopen the case rather than starting over.
